# MathForge identity

An **M** and **F** share one precision-cut silhouette. Its rising diagonal echoes
a radical sign, while the small amber diamond suggests a spark from the forge.

- [Primary mark](mark.svg) — transparent, two colors; suitable for light and dark backgrounds.
- [Single-color mark](mark-mono.svg) — uses `currentColor` when embedded inline.
- [Preview](logo-preview.png) — light, dark, monochrome, and small-size applications.

Violet `#8B7CF8` · Amber `#F9B85F`

Both SVGs use a `256 × 256` viewBox and two filled paths. They contain no fonts,
raster images, filters, scripts, or external resources. Preserve their aspect
ratio and built-in clear space. Use the mark at 24 px or larger to retain the spark.
